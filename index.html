    What can you actually automate?

    Most repetitive work that happens on a computer: finding and contacting leads, booking confirmations and reminders, answering common customer questions, sending and chasing invoices, moving data between apps and sending you reports. If you're not sure, describe the task on a call and I'll tell you honestly.

    Who owns what you build?

    You do. It runs in your own n8n, Google and AI accounts. If we stop working together, it keeps running.

    What will it cost me each month?

    You pay the tools directly, usually a few dollars to about $30 a month depending on volume. There's no monthly fee to me unless you want ongoing support.

    I don't have n8n or OpenAI. Is that a problem?

    No. n8n Cloud or a self-hosted copy both work, and I'll guide you through setting up each account before the build starts.

    Will automated emails or messages look like spam?

    No. Everything is sent one-to-one in small daily volumes, written for each person, never mass blasts. That protects your reputation and your sender account.

    How long does a build take?

    5 days for a single automation, 10 days for an AI-powered one and about 14 days for a full agent, including testing on your real data.
